Our Story

The Rocmob Group Comes to India

Rocmob Performance Limited is the Indian entity of the internationally operating Rocmob Group — a performance marketing organisation with a proven track record across global markets.

Recognising the extraordinary growth potential of India's digital economy, the Rocmob Group established its India operations in Kolkata — home to a rapidly growing technology ecosystem in Bidhannagar's Sector V.

Our India practice is purpose-built to serve fast-growing Indian businesses with the same rigorous, ROI-focused methodology that has made Rocmob a trusted name globally — adapted for India's unique consumer behaviour, regional diversity, and digital landscape.

We are not a generic digital agency. We are a performance organisation: every campaign, every strategy, every rupee is engineered around measurable business outcomes.
